HOW TO PLAY

  1. ATTACK Node Omega from the first second. Missiles and strike planes cost ATTACK; tap any enemy on the battlefield, or pick one in STATS, to aim at it.
  2. DEFEND against what Omega sends back in waves. COUNTER MISSILE takes missiles, COUNTER ATTACK PLANES take planes, EMERGENCY COUNTER is a last-second shot. You get 10, 10 and 5 per game; so does Omega.
  3. BREAK Omega. It rebuilds stronger each time it falls; the run ends when your Strike Platform does.

CURRENCY — ATTACK pays for warheads and strike planes, COUNTER for counters. Both trickle in; hits pay back part of their cost.

CONTROLS — drag or arrow keys to look around, tap a missile to follow it, keys 1-6 fire the abilities, R restarts.
