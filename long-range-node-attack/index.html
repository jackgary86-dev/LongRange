HOW TO PLAY

  1. ATTACK from the first second. Pick a target - OMEGA, RADAR or MISSILE on the bar, or tap one on the field - then fire missiles and strike planes (they cost ATTACK).
  2. DEFEND against what Omega sends back in waves. Your ground defenses fire on their own; EMERGENCY COUNTER is yours to fire at any threat in its last 5 seconds. You get 15 per game, and so does Omega.
  3. BREAK Omega. It rebuilds stronger each time it falls; the run ends when your Strike Platform does.

NODES — each base has 3 missile nodes and 2 radar nodes in front of it. Knock out Omega's missile nodes and its waves shrink; knock out its radar and its defenses go blind. Omega hits yours too: lose missile nodes and your income drops, lose radar and you see threats later.

CURRENCY — ATTACK pays for warheads and strike planes. It trickles in, and hits pay back part of their cost.

CONTROLS — drag or arrow keys to look around, tap a missile to follow it, keys 1-3 fire missiles, 4 fires the Emergency Counter, R restarts.
