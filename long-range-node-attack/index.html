Deep space, hostile territory. You command a Strike Platform launching FAST/ MEDIUM/LONG RANGE warheads across a debris-and-nebula corridor at Node Omega, a shielded enemy citadel dug in at the far end. The corridor itself is littered with smaller targets — infantry squads, vehicles, mobile launchers and sub-bases — pick your objective from the OPERATIONS CENTER; soft targets die in a shot or two, but launchers, sub-bases and Omega itself are armed and shoot back. Five anti-missile batteries guard your side of the corridor and try to time an intercept on anything inbound, but they're unreliable — expect them to connect only about 1 shot in 7. A defender can only track two inbound warheads at once, so slower LONG RANGE and CLUSTER shots are easier to hit than FAST ones — send cheap decoys first to fill a defense's hands, then land your real strike behind them. Omega strikes back with its own inbound warheads — when one of its MEDIUM/LARGE strikes closes to within a few seconds of impact, the EMERGENCY COUNTER button (top left) lights up with its speed and time to impact and lets you manually fire up to two more intercepts at it yourself; it can't lock onto FAST strikes. Three token pillars fuel everything — ATTACK (warheads, kills), COUNTER (Counter Missile, Counter Attack Planes, Emergency Counter) and INTEL (recon and hunt-kill drones) — trickling in passively and paid out per hit, intercept, discovery and wave clear; spend what you don't need on ammo toward permanent reactor upgrades instead. Full launch, ballistic flight and impact play out on the tactical scope with the camera auto-following your shot — click any contact in ACTIVE CONTACTS to track it, or drag and use arrow keys to look around yourself.
